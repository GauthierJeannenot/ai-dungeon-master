# Adamantine — XGE2017, PHB2014 and DMG2014

XGE `rules/books/xge.md:986–992` makes a hit against an object critical when its actual melee weapon or projectile is made of or coated with adamantine. It adds500gp to one weapon or ten ammunition pieces. This does not make a miss hit, add magic, change a creature attack, double modifiers or double a natural critical twice. Damage still uses the canonical source weapon dice and the existing DMG object AC/HP, resistance, immunity and threshold.

Material belongs to its actual registered instance. A direct shot removes one unit, loading moves one into its actual host, a separate unload creates one proven physical unit, and a real completed-battle search returns only the explicitly selected actual material shot IDs within the global source half. `recoveredAdamantineAttackIds` must explicitly identify the recovered material units, including an empty array where appropriate. Source unit balances and immutable origins survive SQL, dropping, transfer and merchant custody. None of these operations pays the batch premium again or changes another instance.

An intact weapon uses half its complete retained normal+500gp value: a longsword version515gp resells for257gp5sp, with real accepted condition and available merchant denominations. Partial ammunition material pricing is refused with `ADAMANTINE_SOURCE_BATCH_PRICE_REQUIRED`; no guessed per-unit value, rounding, appraisal, purchase-back stock or erased matter is supplied. Other special premiums/associated effects retain their existing refusal.

Existing silvering and Basic Poison procedures that split ammunition into new identities refuse these material lots with `ADAMANTINE_PROJECTILE_SPLIT_UNSUPPORTED` before payment, dose, action or clock. Their derived material-custody integration remains deferred. Existing one-unit DMG coating procedures that keep the same physical identity remain separate; no free coating, protection, duration reset or additional dose is granted.
